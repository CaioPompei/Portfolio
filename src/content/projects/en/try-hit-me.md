> **Team project**, made by a team of three.

Enemies arrive in waves and get more dangerous over time: tracked robots, mechanical spiders and more. Light is concentrated around the player, so whatever is in the dark is a threat.

## What I built

- **Menus and UI:** main menu, video and audio options, pause, HUD with a health bar and the game over screen.
- **Enemies and weapons:** new enemies, weapon sound effects and wave balancing.
- **Music:** I composed the game's theme music.
- **Releases:** shipped the Alpha 1.0 and 1.2 builds, with bug fixes and quality-of-life improvements.
