A portfolio site for a photographer and videomaker. The home page introduces her work with three fanned-out portraits and leads to a page for each service: photography, video, storymaking and social media management.

## Decisions

- **Her work comes first:** a dark background and quiet typography so the photos and videos stand out.
- **One page per service**, so each can be shared on its own.
- **Direct contact** over WhatsApp on every page.
- **Static Astro** on Vercel, loading fast even with lots of images.
