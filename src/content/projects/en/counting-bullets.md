> **Team project**, made by a team of four for GMTK Game Jam 2026.

A western FPS in a low-poly desert, with hand-drawn enemies such as scorpions and coyotes. A cat in a cowboy hat guides the tutorial, and ammo is the heart of the game: the cylinder holds few bullets and reloading has a cooldown.

## What I built

- **Shooting and ammo:** the firing mechanic with bullet counting and the reload with a cooldown.
- **Scoring:** points per kill, with a headshot bonus and HUD.
- **Map and enemies:** the desert map, enemy navigation, wave spawning and the scorpion's gravity.
- **Mission board:** missions with rewards and their interface.
- **Integration:** merged the team's branches and resolved conflicts on a jam deadline.

## What I learned

In a game jam, scope is the most important decision. Cutting ideas early and keeping the game playable after every merge mattered more than any extra feature.
